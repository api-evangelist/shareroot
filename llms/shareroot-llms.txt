# ShareRoot

> ShareRoot was a user-generated content (UGC) marketing platform and, as ShareRoot Ltd
> (ASX: SRO), an Australian listed group whose brands included the ShareRoot UGC platform,
> The Social Science marketing agency and the Ludomade digital-experience studio. Shareholders
> approved a rename to Opyl Limited (ASX: OPL) at the December 2019 AGM, and the group
> repositioned into AI for clinical trials and digital health. ShareRoot publishes no API,
> no developer portal, no machine-readable specification and no client SDK. This file is
> generated by API Evangelist from the catalog profile; it is not published by ShareRoot.

## Status

- ShareRoot Ltd renamed to Opyl Limited (ASX: OPL); the successor site is https://opyl.ai
- ludomade.com, the group's digital-experience brand, now 301-redirects to opyl.ai
- shareroot.co still resolves but every path answers HTTP 202 with a SiteGround robot
  challenge; the last readable archived snapshots are from 2024
- No api., developer., docs., app. or platform. subdomain of shareroot.co resolves in DNS

## APIs
